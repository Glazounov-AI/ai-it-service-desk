### Architectural decisions

- Keep classification separate from diagnostic decision-making.
- Keep Service Desk business logic outside RAG Assistant.
- Introduce persistent structured diagnostic case state.
- Use confirmed user facts rather than assistant assumptions.
- Use classification for targeted knowledge retrieval.
- Make clarification, resolution and escalation decisions
  through a deterministic Decision Engine.
- Avoid unnecessary LLM calls when application rules are sufficient.
- Preserve SQLite storage for the MVP.
- Coordinate end-to-end request deadlines during F5.
